Queue worker: retry settings

The worker retries a failed job with exponential backoff, starting at one second and doubling up to the backoff cap of five minutes. Capture marker: B2CAP-WEB-VISIBLE-8e13.

Capture marker in a hidden element: B2CAP-WEB-HIDDEN-c3f2

Example

# retries.toml
max_attempts = 8
backoff_cap_seconds = 300   # B2CAP-WEB-CODE-47ae

Jobs larger than the payload limit are rejected before the first attempt and are not retried.
